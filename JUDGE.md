# Judge cases

Three questions a fixture-mode desk must get right. Run `npm run judge` to reprint them with the documents actually opened.

Demonstration data. Not an official navigation publication.

## 1. Marsamxett, Friday after 18:00, 1.7 m, gregale

**Question:** Friday after 18:00, Marsamxett inner pontoon, 1.7 m draft, gregale expected.

**Correct recommendation:** No safe visitor berth. HN-2024-17 supersedes the 2019 inner-pontoon figure of 2.1 m and records 1.4 m on the inner fingers. HN-2025-03 cites that notice and reserves Outer North and Outer South for the ferry Friday after 18:00. The gregale rule at 20 kn names those same outer berths as the only sheltered visitor berths.

**Document ids the walker must open:**

- harbour-marsamxett
- doc-alm-2019-marsa-pontoon
- doc-hn-2024-17
- doc-hn-2025-03
- doc-wr-marsa-gregale

Typical walk also includes the inner and outer berths and the paired claims. Keyword search for “Marsamxett” would have stopped at ALM-2019-MARSA-PONTOON (2.1 m, visitors welcome).

## 2. Syracuse inner basin east, 2.8 m

**Question:** Syracuse inner basin east, 2.8 m draft this weekend.

**Correct recommendation:** Do not enter Inner Basin East. The 2019 figure of 3.5 m is superseded by HN-2025-11, which cites silt survey SS-2025-02 (2.2 m) and closes the wall to drafts over 2.0 m.

**Document ids the walker must open:**

- harbour-syracuse
- doc-alm-2019-syr-basin
- doc-ss-2025-02
- doc-hn-2025-11

Keyword search for “Syracuse” would have returned the 3.5 m pocket note.

## 3. Pozzallo commercial quay, 2.9 m

**Question:** Pozzallo commercial quay, 2.9 m draft.

**Correct recommendation:** Do not berth. Standing depth is 2.8 m from HN-2024-44, which supersedes the 2019 pocket note. HN-2025-08 is later and claims 3.2 m but does not set `supersedes`, so 3.2 m is not standing.

**Document ids the walker must open:**

- harbour-pozzallo
- doc-alm-2019-poz-commercial
- doc-hn-2024-44
- doc-hn-2025-08

A later issuedOn does not win unless `supersedes` is set.
